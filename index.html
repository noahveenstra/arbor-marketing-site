Capture

Catch the thought before it slips away.

Arbor gives every half-formed thought a place to land. Write it down and move on. No folders, tags, or sorting ritual to slow you down.

Recall

Find it from a fragment.

Search a phrase, a subject, or a detail. Arbor brings the note back when you remember only a piece of the moment—not its title or where you filed it.

Private by design

Your archive is yours.

Arbor searches on your device and never sends note text to a third-party AI service. Notes sync through your private iCloud database.

iCloud’s standard data protection encrypts notes in transit and on Apple’s servers; Apple holds the keys. Recently opened notes are cached unencrypted in this browser, including their text. Signing out deletes that cache. It does not delete your iCloud notes.
